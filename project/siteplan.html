Site Plan

Waves and Sand Runners Pisco

This name represents a club for runners who live in the city of Pisco, and it also refers to the waves and the sand because they usually practice running on the beach.

Site Purpose

The site provides information on how the club is organized, the activities such as weekly practices and competitions, as well as a form where they can join the club.

Scenarios

What activities could I do if I join this group?

How is this club organized, and what can I do if I want to get more information?

Color Schema

Color: #255876 (Cyan Blue) I will use this color in the header and footer.

Color: #F2B143 (Yellow) I will use this color in the subtitles.
